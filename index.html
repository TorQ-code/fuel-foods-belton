Coming home

Less than two years ago we had the truck at the Belton community rummage sale. Somebody in line said, "When are y'all opening a restaurant?" A lady behind him answered before we could: "Hopefully in Belton. We need this."

We figured Fuel was a Greenville-and-Anderson brand. Belton, the town we grew up in, kept showing up anyway.

607 N Main is going to be the place you drive to on purpose.
